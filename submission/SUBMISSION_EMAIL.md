### Project Summary
Guardian Wallet is an AI-assisted digital wallet designed to make digital payments more convenient without sacrificing user control. Users describe payment requests in natural language, while a deterministic policy engine checks spending limits, trusted recipients, and security risks before funds can move. A smart-contract vault independently enforces critical spending restrictions on-chain. Additional safeguards include lookalike-address detection, suspicious invoice protection, owner-controlled approvals, a tamper-evident activity log, and an emergency payment freeze.

Our goal is to demonstrate how AI-powered financial interactions can be made safer through enforceable security boundaries, transaction transparency, and user-controlled financial authority.
